About Me

Hello! I'm Dylan Maramba, a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology graduate with experience in web and mobile application development, networking, and computer systems servicing.

I build responsive websites, mobile applications, and database-driven systems using technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, Laravel, Flutter, Firebase, and MySQL. I focus on creating software that is functional, intuitive, and user-friendly.

During my academic journey, I developed applications such as an E-Mobile Voting System, a Music Streaming Application, and a NoteList App. These projects strengthened my skills in software development, database management, authentication, problem-solving, and UI implementation.

I also completed an internship at the Philippine College of Science and Technology, where I provided technical instruction in computer networking and computer systems servicing while assisting with hardware, software, and network troubleshooting.
